# Code of Conduct

SavannaGuard is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community.

Participants should be respectful, assume good faith while remaining evidence-driven, focus criticism on ideas and implementation, protect private information, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberately malicious code.

Good-faith security research is welcome when performed responsibly and legally. Do not use the project or other people's systems for unauthorized activity.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when behaviour violates this code.
